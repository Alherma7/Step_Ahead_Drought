Hi @AJoel @meganomaly,

First, thank you for handling the July data issue so quickly and openly — the flat

CSVs are much easier to work with than the NetCDF stack.

I'd like to re-raise two questions that @ivarick asked on 22 July and @kbkb on

24 July, which I don't think have been answered yet. Both change what a compliant

solution looks like, and I'd much rather rebuild now than find out after the

deadline.

1. Reanalysis covariates dated month t+1.

The relaunch note says no feature may encode data from month t+1 or later,

regardless of source. At the same time, the challenge description says the time

index follows GRACE availability, and that GRACE products arrive with a 2–3 month

delay. So at the moment we are predicting t+1, the real-world date is roughly t+3,

and a reanalysis field for calendar month t+1 would already have been published.

Is the t+1 cutoff absolute in index terms, or does "available at prediction time"

permit reanalysis (e.g. ERA5) for the target month, given that delay?

2. Latitude and longitude as a join key.

The update says lat/lon must not be used as features. Does that also exclude using

them purely as an equality key — to retrieve the same cell's own earlier TWS

history, or to average neighbouring cells' strictly earlier values — with no

coordinate-derived quantity ever entering the model as a predictor?

3. Observed test months.

Confirming @PeterTheAnalyst's reading: for a row at month m, the only compliant use

of the six fully observed test months is the most recent one at or before m — never

a later one, and never a per-cell mean across all of them. Is that correct?

Until we hear otherwise we are treating all three as prohibited and rebuilding on

strictly backward-looking features only. Happy to share specifics if that helps.

Thanks for putting this challenge together.
