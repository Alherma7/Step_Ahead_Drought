The rules state that, for a prediction at calendar month t, only information available at or before t may be used, and that external operational data should be available within one month of acquisition.

ERA5T would satisfy this timing but is not publicly archived for the historical challenge period; it is replaced by ERA5 Final after validation.

May we use publicly available ERA5 Final values for calendar month t as a documented retrospective proxy for ERA5T, provided that no atmospheric variable after t, and no future GRACE/TWS information, is used ?

We would fully document the source, variables, dates and the proxy assumption.
